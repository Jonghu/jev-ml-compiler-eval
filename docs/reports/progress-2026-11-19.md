## Primary findings

| Benchmark / metric | Jev | Random | Better |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| TenSet ResNet-50: network Top-1 score | 0.0977 | 0.0915 | Higher |
| TenSet ResNet-50: network Top-5 score | 0.2746 | 0.3771 | Higher |
| TpuGraphs R7: mean Top-1 error | 413.6% | 268.4% | Lower |
| TpuGraphs R7: mean Top-5 error | 48.9% | 57.6% | Lower |

Jev shows mixed selection quality: a fragile TenSet Top-1 lead and weaker Top-5, but lower TpuGraphs Top-5/10 errors despite worse mean Top-1. Coarse probabilities and poor finalist selection appear in both analyses. TenSet's audit reproduces all 108 endpoint decompositions; 95.3% of 100,000 offline Random draws beat its fixed Jev Top-5 result. This is conditional Random variability, not a p-value or repeated Jev experiment. Published MLP/GNN scores are external references; matching checkpoints were not trained or run locally.
